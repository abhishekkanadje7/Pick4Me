Step-by-Step Flow

How Pick4Me Works

A simple 4-step process designed for fast, trusted and hassle-free local peer delivery.

📝

1. Create a Request

Tell us what product you need, specify the preferred shop name, destination address, estimated price, and the delivery reward.

👀

2. Find a Shopper

Nearby students and community members browsing available requests can claim your order with a single click.

🛒

3. Shopper Picks It Up

The shopper visits the shop, purchases your item, and updates the live delivery progress on the platform.

📦

4. Get It Delivered

The shopper delivers the product to your door. You inspect the item, confirm delivery, and pay easily via demo UPI QR!

About Pick4Me

Connecting Neighbors & Students

"Pick4Me is built around a simple idea — if someone nearby needs something from a shop and another person is already going there, they can help each other."

Pick4Me is specially designed for college campuses, hostels, residential communities, and local neighbourhoods. The goal is to make small local shopping easier while giving shoppers a simple, flexible earning opportunity.
